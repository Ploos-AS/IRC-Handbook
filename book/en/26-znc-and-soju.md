# ZNC and soju: two bouncer models

This book covers both **ZNC** and **soju**.

ZNC is an established IRC bouncer with a module ecosystem and a long deployment history. It is useful for learning the traditional bouncer model while also providing features beyond a simple relay.

soju is designed around modern IRC and IRCv3 concepts and is especially interesting for multi-client use, history and modern protocol features.

Rather than declaring one universally correct choice, the labs build both and compare installation, configuration, TLS, upstream SASL, multiple networks, multiple clients, history/playback, IRCv3 support, backup and upgrades.
