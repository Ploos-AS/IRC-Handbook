# From BNC to modern bouncers

Bouncers have a long history in IRC shell culture. Classic environments commonly combined screen, Irssi, BNC or psyBNC, and Eggdrop on a shell account.

Early bouncers primarily focused on maintaining an IRC connection and relaying a user's later client connection through it.

psyBNC is historically useful for understanding this generation of software. Historical popularity, however, is not by itself a reason to deploy old software today; current maintenance, security and protocol support must be evaluated separately.

Modern bouncers may add multiple networks, multiple devices, message history, IRCv3 integration, user management, TLS and upstream SASL. The bouncer has therefore evolved from a simple relay into a richer layer of IRC infrastructure.
