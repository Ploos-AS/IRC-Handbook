# Eggdrop, EnergyMech, Dancer and Sopel

IRC has a long bot tradition, and this book covers several families to show different eras and design approaches.

**Eggdrop** is strongly associated with classic channel and Unix shell culture.

**EnergyMech** is another representative of the classic IRC bot world and is useful when studying long-running shell-hosted bots and channel administration.

**Dancer** is another historical bot name worth recognizing in existing IRC environments.

**Sopel** represents a more modern extensible model in which functionality can be organized as plugins.

The goal is not to make every reader deploy every bot. The goal is to recognize classic shell bots, channel-management bots, plugin-oriented systems, legacy installations and custom-purpose bots, while understanding the IRC protocol they share.
